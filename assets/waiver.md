# Alpine Club Liability Waiver and Release

**Please read this waiver carefully before signing. By accepting, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by its terms.**

---

## Assumption of Risk

I, the undersigned member ("Member"), acknowledge and agree that **alpine and backcountry activities** — including but not limited to ski touring, mountaineering, ice climbing, rock climbing, snowshoeing, and backcountry travel — involve inherent risks that cannot be entirely eliminated. These risks include, but are not limited to:

- Avalanche, rockfall, and other natural hazards
- Severe or rapidly changing weather conditions
- Terrain features including steep slopes, cliffs, crevasses, and unstable snow
- Physical injury or death
- Equipment failure
- The actions of other participants or third parties
- Emergencies far from medical assistance

I freely and voluntarily accept and assume all such risks, both known and unknown, and assume full responsibility for any injury, loss, damage, or expense arising from my participation in Club activities.

---

## Release of Liability

In consideration of being permitted to participate in activities organized by the **Alpine Club** ("the Club"), I hereby **release, waive, discharge, and covenant not to sue** the Club, its directors, officers, employees, volunteers, agents, sponsors, and other participants from any and all liability, claims, demands, or causes of action that I may have or that may arise in the future, whether foreseeable or unforeseeable, relating to my participation in Club activities, even if caused in whole or in part by the negligence of the Club or any of the released parties.

---

## Medical Authorization

I represent that I am in good physical condition and have no medical conditions that would prevent my safe participation in Club activities. I consent to emergency medical treatment in the event I am unable to consent for myself during a Club activity. I acknowledge that the Club does not provide medical insurance and that I am responsible for my own medical costs.

---

## Rules and Conduct

I agree to:

1. Follow all instructions given by trip leaders and guides.
2. Abide by all applicable laws, regulations, and Club policies.
3. Not participate in any Club activity while under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
4. Immediately report any injury, hazard, or unsafe condition to the trip leader.
5. Behave in a manner that does not endanger myself or others.

---

## Indemnification

I agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Club and its officers, directors, employees, volunteers, and agents from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising out of or resulting from my participation in Club activities or any breach of this agreement.

---

## Photography and Media

I grant the Club permission to photograph and/or video record me during Club activities and to use such images for Club publications, website, and promotional materials without compensation.

---

## Severability

If any provision of this waiver is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in full force and effect.

---

## Acknowledgement

By clicking **I Accept**, I confirm that:

- I am **18 years of age or older**, or a legal guardian has authorized my participation.
- I have **read this entire waiver**, understand its terms, and agree to be bound by them.
- I understand that this waiver **significantly affects my legal rights**.
- I am signing this waiver **voluntarily and of my own free will**.

*This waiver is valid for one year from the date of acceptance and must be renewed annually.*
